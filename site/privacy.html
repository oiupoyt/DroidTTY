# privacy policy

For the DroidTTY Android app (space.oiupoyt.droidtty) and this website. Effective 27 November 2026.

in short

DroidTTY collects nothing. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it has no server of its own. Everything it reads on your phone is used on the phone to draw your home screen, always-on display, lock screens and notification shade, and nothing of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

what the app reads, and why

Each of these is asked for only when you switch on the feature that needs it, and can be withdrawn in Android's settings at any time.

accessused for
installed appsthe app list and drawer; names of apps in the readout.
usage accessscreen time per app, app switches and data use in the readout.
notification accessyour notifications in DroidTTY's shade and log, and what's playing. Read when posted, held in memory while the app runs, never written to storage.
accessibility service ("lock service")drawing the always-on display, lock screens and shade over other apps and the lock screen; turning the screen off on a double-tap; seeing which kind of window opened and its size, to close Android's shade or put the lock back; blocking the home, back and recents keys while DroidTTY's own PIN lock is up. It never reads what is on screen or what you type.
locationthe GPS fix and satellite rows, only while such a row is shown on the home screen.
phone statecell signal and network type rows, and staying out of the way during calls.
physical activitythe step count row, from the phone's step sensor.
nearby devices (Bluetooth), Wi-Fi statethe Bluetooth and Wi-Fi panels in the shade, network rows.
display over other apps, notificationsthe always-on display and boot screen when the lock service is off; update notices.
Shizuku (optional, a separate app)processes, connections per app, wakelocks, battery use and system errors in the system monitor.

what is stored, and where

Only on your phone, in DroidTTY's private storage: your settings and presets, fonts you import, the last crash report, the step counter's daily baseline and the time of the last update check. A PIN for DroidTTY's own lock is stored only as a salted PBKDF2 hash, in storage that Android never backs up. If you use Android's backup to your Google account, Android may include DroidTTY's settings in it; that backup is between you and Google.

network use

  • Update check (once a day, can be switched off in settings › about). The GitHub and F-Droid builds download one small public file, version.json, from this project's GitHub releases. The Google Play build asks Google Play whether an update is available. No information about you or your phone is included; GitHub or Google receive your IP address and the app's version as part of the request, under their own privacy policies. In the GitHub build, tapping the update downloads the new APK from GitHub.
  • DNS monitor (optional, off by default). A VPN that runs only on your phone: it sees which hostnames your apps look up, shows them in the readout, and forwards each lookup to your network's own DNS server. No traffic goes to any server of ours; there isn't one.
  • Hostnames in the connections table (optional, with Shizuku). The addresses apps connect to are looked up through your phone's normal DNS server to show a name.

sharing

Nothing is shared. A crash report only leaves the phone if you share it yourself with Android's share menu, to wherever you choose.

children

DroidTTY isn't directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

this website

droidtty.oiupoyt.space is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes visitors' IP addresses to serve and protect the site under its privacy policy. The site sets no cookies and has no analytics. The colour you pick is kept in your browser's local storage and never sent anywhere.

changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page and in the project's history on GitHub. Questions: open an issue on GitHub or Forgejo.
