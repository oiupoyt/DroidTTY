Your Android phone, as a terminal.

A launcher that puts your apps in front of a live readout of the phone: processes, connections, battery, network. Around it, an always-on display in animated ASCII, lock screens that boot like a PC, and a notification shade drawn in boxes.

download the apk source on github

free · open source (GPL-3.0) · android 8.0+ · no ads, no tracking

# every screen

Six parts of the phone, all in the same terminal. Pick one; the phone turns to it. Screens are drawn here in HTML after the app's own layout; the always-on and boot ones play the app's real frames.

home: pinned apps as text, in front of the readout. swipe up for the drawer, down for the shade.

# what's in it

the readout

52 status rows you pick and order: cpu clocks and load, memory, thermal headroom, battery current in watts, network speed, wi-fi and cell signal, gps fix, sunrise, moon phase. An event stream logs network changes, installs and app switches.

cpu   8 cores  0.60-2.85 GHz  schedutil
bat   76%  discharging  31.2°C  good
net   wifi  ↓1.2M/s ↑48K/s
mem   3.1G / 7.5G available

system monitor

With Shizuku: processes by cpu, every connection per app with server names, wakelocks, battery drain per app, temperatures, system errors. Phones where Shizuku can't start a helper fall back to its own shell by themselves.

always-on display

Doom fire, starfield, game of life, a spinning cube, rain, an aquarium, an oscilloscope, pong, plasma, a dna helix and matrix rain, plus still looks like neofetch and htop. Size, brightness, colour, position and timeout are yours; it goes dark in a pocket.

lock screens that boot

Ten presets built from your phone's real kernel, memory and battery: a full arch linux boot, a bios flash, dos, a getty login, grub, a commodore 64, a kernel panic, ssh. Over android's own lock, or as droidtty's own pin lock with a shuffled keypad.

its own shade

Notifications in ascii boxes with reply, snooze and actions. Toggles for wi-fi, bluetooth, data, nfc, hotspot, battery saver and more; brightness and volume sliders; bluetooth devices with battery; what's playing.

dressed as a crt

Eleven presets (green p1, amber, paper, ember, deep space, matrix...) each with a matching always-on look and lock screen. Eleven bundled fonts or your own; glow, scanlines, curvature, vignette, colour fringe, flicker and grain.

    1. install Open the apk and allow "install unknown apps" for your browser. Play Protect warns about apps that use accessibility and notification access; choose more details › install anyway.
    2. make it home Settings › apps › default apps › home app › DroidTTY.
    3. get started Long-press the home screen, open get started: each switch DroidTTY needs opens the exact android setting.
    4. updates DroidTTY checks once a day and offers new versions itself; you can switch that off.
